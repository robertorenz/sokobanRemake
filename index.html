How to play

You are the warehouse keeper. Push every crate onto a goal marker. You can only push crates – never pull – and only one at a time. Crates turn green when they sit on a goal.

Levels 1–50 are the original Sokoban levels by Thinking Rabbit (1982–84); 51–90 are the classic "Extra" set. Your progress is saved in this browser.
